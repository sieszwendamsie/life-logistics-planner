# Life logistics planner

A personal logistics planning system for life and hybrid work across Poznań and Italy.
It plans stays, office vs remote days, travel itineraries and holidays, and proposes a
few realistic options to compare instead of a single answer.

**Status:** early setup. No features yet.

## Goal

Cut planning the next stay from several hours to about 15 minutes of reviewing and
approving proposed options. See [docs/mvp.md](docs/mvp.md) for the MVP scope.

## Principles

- Search, compare and propose on its own. Never book or pay without explicit approval.
- Reuse existing tools (Google Calendar, Maps, flight and stay search) before building new ones.
